What the game files carry

The viewer builds the game in your tab from your US Emerald dump. Two files do it: the module patch, the file that turns your dump into the game the viewer runs, and Emscripten's glue, the script that starts that game. Both files carry code from these projects:

  • Emscripten 7.0.0: the glue is Emscripten's runtime. Emscripten offers the MIT licence and the University of Illinois/NCSA Open Source License.
  • musl: its formatted printing and stdio writers, linked into the module, under the MIT licence.
  • Emscripten's setjmp, longjmp and stack helpers, linked into the module, under the MIT and NCSA licences.
  • pokeemerald-expansion: it publishes no licence, so its authors keep all rights to it. The patches carry its compiled code, as the project's decision records ADR 0002 and ADR 0007 state.

The emulator page

The emulator page runs mGBA, under the Mozilla Public License 2.0 (MPL-2.0). Its source: https://github.com/mgba-emu/mgba. The page loads @thenick775/mgba-wasm 2.6.0, built from https://github.com/thenick775/mgba, MPL-2.0 as well.

The battle data

The pages read battle logs, species and moves through the @pkmn libraries @pkmn/client, @pkmn/data, @pkmn/dex and @pkmn/protocol, under the MIT licence. Their source: https://github.com/pkmn/ps.
