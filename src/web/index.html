Ingame Replay Viewer

Watch a Pokémon Showdown replay play out inside Pokémon Emerald. The page builds the game from your own US Emerald dump, then plays the replay you pick: an example, a replay link, or a saved replay file.

Your dump and your replay files stay in this tab. The page uploads nothing.

The viewer plays Gen 1 to 3 singles for now.

The game runs in your browser as WebAssembly, without an emulator. If it fails here, try the emulator page.
